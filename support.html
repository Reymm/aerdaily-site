Support

The fastest way to reach us is email: help@aerdaily.com. AERDaily is built by one person, a fellow patient, so replies can take a day or two.

Is AERDaily free?

Yes. Free for everyone, and the core app will stay free. There is an optional tip jar for people who want to help cover the running costs. Nothing you need is locked behind it.

Where does my data live?

On your phone. An account is optional and exists only so you can back up and sync your own logs. We never sell data and we never share it with advertisers. See the privacy policy for the full picture.

Is this medical advice?

No. AERDaily is an education and tracking tool. It never tells you to start, stop, or change a medication. Talk to your own doctor about treatment decisions.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, Settings has two options: Delete my account, and Delete everything on this phone. Either one works right away, no email needed.

Chip in, if you can

AERDaily is free, and it will stay free. I built it for our community because we needed it, and I will keep building it either way. It does cost a bit to keep it hosted and running, so if the app has helped you and you are able to chip in now and then, it truly helps and I am grateful.

If you cannot, please do not worry for a second. Use the app, share it with someone who needs it, and that is support enough.

Thank you for being here. Matt
