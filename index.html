A free app for people living with AERD, also called Samter's Triad.

Living with AERD is an every day thing.
Now you can track it like one.

Daily check ins, injection logs with reminders, a food and drink checker, and a plain language library. Built by someone who lives with it.

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play  ·  help@aerdaily.com

Daily check in

Congestion, smell, taste, asthma, sleep, rescue inhaler use. Thirty seconds each morning, and your patterns start to show.

Injections and reminders

Log every biologic shot, where it went, and what showed up after it. Set a reminder so the next one never sneaks up on you.

Can I have this?

Look up foods, drinks, and common meds before you guess. Built around the low omega 6 way of eating our community follows.

Learn

Treatments, myths, and the things doctors do not always mention, written in plain language. Budesonide soaks included.

Made by a patient, for patients

AERDaily is built by Matt Rey, an electrician from Saskatchewan who was diagnosed with Samter's Triad in 2017. It started as a question in the Samter's Society Facebook group, and the members there asked for most of what is in it.

The app is free and it will stay free. Your logs live on your phone. Your data is never sold, to anyone, ever.

Be first to know when AERDaily launches

Join the launch list. One email when the app lands in the App Store and Google Play, and a short newsletter now and then after that. No spam, your address is never sold, and you can unsubscribe anytime.
