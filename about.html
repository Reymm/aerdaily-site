About me

I'm Matt. I was diagnosed with Samter's Triad in 2017. I live in a small town in Saskatchewan with my wife and our two kids, and I work full time as an electrician.

If you have this, you already know the shape of it. The congestion that never fully clears. The polyps that keep coming back. The smell and taste that left and took a lot with them.

Trying to explain it to people is its own problem. Most people have never heard of it. You tell someone you cannot take a normal painkiller, or that one glass of wine can put you in a bad way for days, and they look at you like you are exaggerating. You look fine. So people nod, and they do not really get it, and after a while you stop trying to explain. That is part of why this app exists. Here, nobody needs the explanation.

I remember when my second child was born. Everyone talks about that clean baby smell after a bath. I could not smell it. I held my own son and got nothing, and I was upset in a way I did not expect. Going out to eat was the same. Everyone else enjoying their food while I sat there guessing. It wears on you. Life is busy, everyone has their own stuff going on, and this sits underneath all of it, quiet, every day. If any of that sounds familiar, you are not alone. That is the whole reason this app exists.

I am currently on Dupixent and my symptoms have subsided. I know that is not everyone's story, and I know treatments change. I will do my best to keep this app updated and keep improving it.

The honest part: I work away from my family 14 days at a time and I get 6 days home with them. That is when most of the work on this app happens, so updates may not be fast, but they will come.

This app is free and it will stay free. It does cost money to host and maintain, so if you are able to chip in and help keep it running, I appreciate it more than I can say. If you cannot, no worries at all. Use it. That is what it is here for.

Matt
