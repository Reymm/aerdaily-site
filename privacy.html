AERDaily Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

AERDaily is a free app for people living with AERD (Samter's Triad), built by Matt Rey, a fellow patient. This policy explains what happens to your information in plain language.

The short version

Your health logs live on your phone. We do not sell your data. We do not show ads. We do not use trackers. An account is optional and only exists so you can back up and sync your own logs.

What the app stores on your phone

Everything you log stays on your device by default:

  • Daily check ins (congestion, smell, taste, asthma, sleep, rescue inhaler use, triggers)
  • Injection and medication logs, including sites, dates, and reactions
  • Peak flow readings and flare logs
  • Food, alcohol, and symptom notes
  • Your profile and reminder settings

You can delete all of it at any time in Settings, using "Delete everything on this phone." Deleting the app also removes it.

If you choose to create an account

An account is optional. The app works fully without one. If you create a free account (with Apple, Google, or email), your logs are copied to a secure server so you can back them up and sync them between your devices. That copy is yours. It is used for backup and sync only.

You can delete your account at any time in Settings, using "Delete my account." That permanently deletes the server copy as well.

What we never do

  • We never sell your personal or health information to anyone, for any reason.
  • We never share it with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We never use outside advertising or analytics trackers.
  • We never look at your logs except if you ask us for help and send them to us yourself.

Notifications

Injection and medication reminders are optional and off unless you turn them on. They are generated on your phone. Turning them off stops them completely.

Email

If you email our help address, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to answer you.

Donations and affiliate links

The app is free. If you choose to tip or donate, that payment is handled by the payment provider, and we receive only what they normally report to a seller, never your card details. Some pages may link to products with affiliate links, always clearly labelled. Tapping a link is your choice, and the shop's own privacy policy applies once you leave the app.

Children

AERDaily is made for adults living with AERD. It is not directed at children under 13.

Not medical advice

AERDaily is an education and tracking tool. It does not diagnose, treat, or give medical advice, and it never tells you to start, stop, or change a medication. Always talk to your own doctor about treatment decisions.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will say so inside the app before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions about privacy: help@aerdaily.com
